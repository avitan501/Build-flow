# Avantia contractor discovery — 30-minute conversation

I’d like to understand how material purchasing works on your jobs, where it gets difficult, and what you would actually want help with. We’ll use a recent order, then agree on one useful next step.

Use a recent real order. Ask neutral questions before offering services. Capture the contractor’s own words. Discuss one or two departments, not all 17. Unknown is a valid answer.

4. **Which part took the most effort or did not go as expected?**

9. **Who should confirm quantities and specifications, approve spending, and check the delivery on site?**
   Name each role. Avantia coordinates approved purchases; the contractor/team receives and checks the materials.

11. **What would you hand over, and what would you prefer to keep handling yourself?**

12. **If we could take only one or two recurring tasks off your plate, which would you choose?**

13. **What would make the first order feel successful enough to use us again?**
   Choose an observable result: fewer GC calls, less GC time, a complete approved order or a like-for-like price comparison. No guaranteed savings.

## 27–30 min — Recap & next step

14. **Have I understood the problem and the work you want us to take over correctly?**
   Read back: problem, department, Avantia action, GC responsibility and success measure. Correct the summary together.
   Notes: __________________________

15. **What is the next real order we could start with, and what would you need before proceeding?**
   List/plan/quote needed, person responsible, due date, expected service scope and fee agreement. An interview is not approval to buy.

## Optional follow-ups — use only where relevant

### Takeoff & material cost check
Who produces and verifies the list today, and which quantity or subcontractor inclusion was unclear on the last job?

### Get supplier quotes
How did you compare the last quotes, including matching specifications, delivery and terms?

### Order through Avantia
Which supplier conversations, payments or arrangements did you personally handle on the last order?

### Shortages & reorders
Tell me about the last missing item: who identified it, approved it and arranged the reorder?

## Help plan to review together
- Contractor / project / date
- One or two priority problems, in the contractor’s words
- Relevant departments and one real example
- Frequency and impact: reported estimate / verified / unknown
- Selected Avantia services and specific work to take over
- Who approves quantities, specifications and purchases
- Who checks delivered materials on site
- Excluded work and unanswered questions
- First real order, next action, owner and due date
- Success measure and baseline, if known
- Scope and fee: agreed / still pending

The plan is a proposal for review, not a purchase approval or guaranteed saving. Keep internal service-effort and profitability notes out of the customer version.
